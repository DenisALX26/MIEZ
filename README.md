# M.I.E.Z

**M.I.E.Z** is an all-in-one business management platform designed for small and medium companies. It brings HR, Sales, Inventory, and IT operations under a single roof — with role-based dashboards, workflow automation, and a built-in AI assistant that understands your business context.

---

## What can you do with M.I.E.Z?

### HR Management
Track your workforce end-to-end. Manage employee profiles, departments, contracts, payroll, and attendance. Approve or reject leave requests, monitor upcoming events (birthdays, contract expirations), and generate AI-powered HR digest reports with actionable workforce insights.

### Sales & Orders
Stay on top of your revenue. Create and track orders across multiple channels (direct, website, eMAG), manage customers by tier (Gold / Silver / Bronze), issue invoices, and visualise revenue trends, top products, and channel splits on a live dashboard.

### Inventory
Know exactly what you have and what you need. Track products by SKU, monitor stock levels with low-stock alerts, log every movement (inbound deliveries, outbound shipments, write-offs, adjustments), and manage your supplier relationships — all in one place.

### IT Support
Handle internal IT requests with a lightweight ticketing system. Tickets are prioritised, assigned, and tracked from open to resolved. Get a real-time view of system health and ticket volume trends.

### Workflow Automation
Build business automations without code. Define triggers — like a stock level dropping below minimum or a new IT ticket being raised — and attach actions such as emailing a supplier, notifying a manager, or generating a report. Every run is logged for full auditability.

### AI Assistant
Every user has access to a conversational AI assistant that knows their role and the live state of the system. Ask it to pull a sales summary, check who's on leave, queue a report, or explain a ticket — it responds in Romanian or English and only acts within the permissions of the logged-in user.

### Reports
Generate structured reports on demand across Sales, Finance, Inventory, HR, and IT. Reports are stored and downloadable at any time.

---

## Who is it for?

M.I.E.Z is built around five roles, each with a tailored view of the platform:

| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| CEO | Cross-department KPIs, HR summary, revenue overview |
| HR Manager | Workforce, attendance, leave, payroll, contracts |
| Sales Rep | Orders, customers, invoices, sales performance |
| Inventory Manager | Products, stock, suppliers, movements |
| IT Technician | Support tickets, system status |

---

## Tech at a glance

Django + Django REST Framework backend, React + TypeScript frontend, PostgreSQL database, deployed on AWS EC2 via Docker. AI features powered by Anthropic Claude.
